How it works

  1. Connect Jobber. Next Jobs reads your jobs, quotes and clients to learn what you sell and where you work. You can correct both.
  2. Permits get matched. Same trade near you, the work that follows (a new roof means gutters), and competitor jobs on blocks where you already have clients.
  3. They land in Jobber. Each one is a new request with the address, the county owner name, the year built and why it matched. Lead source: Next Jobs.

The honest part

A permit usually means that trade's job is already sold. Next Jobs says so on every request. The value is the work after it, the neighbors who just watched a crew on their block, and knowing which competitor is working your streets.
