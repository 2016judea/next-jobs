What the service does

Next Jobs reads public building-permit records from the City of Minneapolis and property records from Hennepin County, matches them to the trades and service area of a connected Jobber account, and creates clients and requests in that account.

What it doesn't promise

Permits are public records published by the city, often days after issue. A permit usually means the permitted work is already sold. Next Jobs doesn't promise that any match becomes work, that the records are complete or correct, or that an owner wants to be contacted. You decide whether and how to follow up, and you follow the law when you do, including do-not-call and solicitation rules.

Billing

The first 7 days of matches are free. After that the service is $29 a month, billed by Stripe, until you cancel. Cancelling stops new matches at the end of the paid month. Requests already created stay in your Jobber.

Your account

You can disconnect at any time from Jobber's App Marketplace or from your account page. Disconnecting deletes the tokens we hold for your Jobber account.

Liability

The service is provided as is. Our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the last three months.
