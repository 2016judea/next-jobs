Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026. Questions: aidan@brickandmortar.dev.

What we read from your Jobber

Your account name and industry; the titles and line-item names of your jobs, quotes and requests; and the map coordinates of your client properties. We use these only to work out what you sell and where you work.

What we store

Your Jobber account id and name, an encrypted access token, the trades and service area we learned (a centre point, a radius, and the coordinates of your client properties), your settings, and a list of the permits we sent you. We don't store your clients' names, emails or phone numbers.

What we write to your Jobber

A client (named from the Hennepin County owner record, or "Homeowner"), a property, a request and a note for each match. Nothing else.

What we never do

We don't sell or share your data, contact your clients, or contact homeowners. When you disconnect, we delete your tokens; ask and we'll delete the rest.

Public records

Permit and property data come from the City of Minneapolis and Hennepin County open-data services.
